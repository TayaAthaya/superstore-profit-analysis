# Superstore Profit Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes sales and profitability data from a retail superstore to
understand how different product categories and sub-categories contribute to
overall business performance. The primary goal is to identify categories that
generate high sales but suffer from low profitability and to uncover the root
causes behind these patterns.

Proyek ini bertujuan untuk menganalisis data penjualan dan profit dari sebuah
retail superstore guna memahami kontribusi setiap kategori dan sub-category
terhadap performa bisnis secara keseluruhan, khususnya dalam hal profitabilitas.

---

## Business Questions
- Which product categories generate the highest sales and profit?
- Are there categories with high sales but low profit margins?
- What factors contribute to low profitability?
- Which sub-categories are the main drivers of losses?
- How do discount strategies impact profit performance?

---

## Dataset
- **Source:** Superstore Sales Dataset (Kaggle)
- **Data Type:** Transaction-level retail data
- **Key Columns:** Sales, Profit, Discount, Category, Sub-Category, Quantity, Order Date

---

## Analysis Approach
The analysis was conducted using a structured, step-by-step approach:
1. Analyze total sales and profit by category.
2. Calculate profit margin to evaluate efficiency.
3. Analyze average discount by category.
4. Drill down into Furniture sub-categories to identify loss drivers.
5. Perform transaction-level analysis on the Tables sub-category.
6. Formulate data-driven business recommendations.

---

## Key Insights
- Technology is the most profitable category due to higher profit margins.
- Furniture generates relatively high sales but has the lowest profit margin.
- Furniture has the highest average discount among all categories.
- The Tables sub-category is the primary contributor to losses within Furniture.
- High and frequent discounts on Tables lead to consistently negative profit per transaction.

---

## Business Recommendations
- Optimize discount strategies for the Furniture category, especially Tables.
- Reduce aggressive discounting and apply discounts more selectively.
- Monitor profit impact after discount adjustments to ensure margin improvement
  without significantly reducing sales volume.
